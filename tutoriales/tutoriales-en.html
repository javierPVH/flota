Quick guide

Learn to use the fleet app on your phone

From your phone you log your car’s kilometres and consumption, report a breakdown or an accident, upload documents and see what you have pending. If you supervise cars, you also run your fleet from here.

Each section shows you step by step with real screenshots of the app. At the top right you can choose whether you drive, supervise or both, and you will only see what applies to you.

Sign in to the app

With the link your company gave you, your username and your password.

  1. On your phone, open the field app link the company gave you. It also comes in the reminder emails, in the Open the field app button.
  2. Enter your username or email and your password and tap Sign in.
  3. At the top right you can change the language: ES / EN.
  4. If it asks, allow notifications: that way it will remind you of what you have pending.
For security, your session closes by itself after 2 hours, or sooner if you leave it unused for a while. And it can only be open on one device at a time: if you sign in from another one, the first one is signed out.
Do you sign in and it says you don’t have access? That’s because you don’t have a car assigned yet or your user doesn’t have permission for this app. Request access from that same screen or talk to administration.

Log your kilometres

Once a month, what the car’s dashboard shows.

Driver
  1. Tap Km on the bottom bar. “Log kilometres” opens with your car’s plate.
  2. At the top it reminds you of the last reading and its date: the new one must be the same or higher.
  3. The reading date is already set to today.
  4. In Odometer (total km on the dashboard) enter the kilometres the car shows, without dots.
  5. Tap Save reading.
When? On the Current km card you will see the best day to log the km for the month. While the reading is missing you will get a reminder email.
Check the figure carefully before saving: one digit too many or too few changes the kilometre calculations for the car’s contract.

Note your average consumption

The consumption shown by the on-board computer.

Driver
  1. Check the on-board computer for the average consumption of your last trip or refuelling cycle (l/100 km, or kWh/100 km if it is electric).
  2. Tap Fuel. “Average consumption” opens.
  3. Enter the figure in Actual average consumption at that moment (for example 6.4). The date is already filled in.
  4. Tap Save consumption. Below you will see your last entry.
Do not note the “historical consumption” or the vehicle’s total accumulated figure: only the one for the last trip. You don’t need to enter litres or amounts.

Log the MOT and maintenance

Once you have passed the MOT or had the service done, you record it here.

Driver

MOT

  1. Tap MOT. At the top you will see the scheduled next MOT.
  2. Enter the MOT date (the Today button fills it in).
  3. Choose the result: Passed closes the MOT alerts; Failed leaves the appointment pending.
  4. If you can, add the MOT report (photo or PDF) and tap Log MOT.

Maintenance

  1. Tap Maintenance. You will see the car’s plan (for example General service), how often it is due and when the next one is.
  2. Once it is done, tap Mark as done and fill in the date and the km.
These buttons can be used from 30 days before the appointment. If it is not due yet, the button looks greyed out: tap it anyway and it will tell you when the appointment is and from what day you can log it.
Appointments are scheduled by administration or your supervisor. If your car has no MOT scheduled, the MOT button does not appear.

A breakdown, a tyre change, one-off maintenance or any other request to fleet management.

Driver

Only the last tab sends it.

“Incident” tab: what is happening

This is the first and most important one: here you say what is happening and how urgent it is.

FieldWhat to enter
Type RequiredWhat kind of incident it is (see the table below). It decides the tabs that come next.
Date RequiredWhen it happened. It comes set to today and does not accept future dates.
Priority RequiredHow urgent it is for you: Critical, Moderate, Functional or Informative. By default, Moderate. Management sorts its inbox by it.
Description RequiredExplain it in your own words: what you notice, since when, whether any warning lights are on… For a Tyre change it doesn’t appear here: it goes in its own tab and is optional.

Below the priority, an amber box explains the type you chose, so you get it right:

BreakdownThe vehicle has failed: it is not roadworthy or cannot be used normally.
One-off maintenanceBroken things or needed changes that do not prevent driving; handled without urgency. Scheduled maintenance goes through its plan, not here.
Tyre changeDue to wear or a puncture. The workshop and appointment are set later, by management.
General requestRequests that may not be related to the vehicle: paperwork, cards, questions…
To continue: type, date, priority and description filled in. If anything is missing, Continue is greyed out.
Was it an accident? Don’t report it here: use Accident, which has its own report.

“Availability” tab: how the car is left

It only appears for Breakdown and One-off maintenance. Choose one of the three cards:

Still in serviceI can keep driving it while it is handled. (This one is selected by default.)
Cannot be usedThe car stays off the road, but no other one is needed.
I need a replacement carA car request is opened for administration, who decide whether to provide one. You must attach a document in the next tab.
What you choose here does not change the vehicle’s state in the app: management records it, and they decide whether the car goes to “Off the road - Broken down” or “Off the road - Maintenance”.

“Tyres” tab: the tyre report

It only appears if the type is Tyre change, and it takes the place of “Availability”. At the top it reminds you what is required.

FieldWhat to enter
Current mileage RequiredIt comes with the car’s last reading. If you have driven since then, correct it.
Reason for replacement RequiredWear or Puncture. The fields below change depending on what you choose.
Which tyres? (wear)Front, Rear or All 4 tyres, and the sizes for each axle chosen.
Which tyre? (puncture)Front or rear, left or right, and the size of that tyre.
CommentOptional: anything that helps the workshop.
The size is written on the side of the tyre, in this format: 205/55 R16.

“Documents” tab: the proof

Attach whatever helps explain it: photos of the damage, the workshop quote… Photos and PDFs are accepted, and you can add several.

  1. Tap Attach a document or photo: you can take the photo there and then or choose it from your gallery.
  2. Each file appears in a list with its bin in case you make a mistake.
  3. It is usually optional: you can continue without attaching anything.
If you asked for a replacement car, attaching at least one document is required. And a second box appears, “Replacement car paperwork”, to upload the papers for the car you have already been given (registration certificate, technical sheet, insurance…). That one is optional: you usually won’t have it yet.
Photos are compressed automatically before uploading, so they don’t use much data.

“Management” tab: where it gets fixed

The last tab. It helps management find you a workshop.

FieldWhat to enter
Preferred location postal codeIf you know it, the postcode you would rather go to the nearest workshop from (5 digits). It is optional: you can leave it blank and it is filled in when the incident is closed.
  1. Go back with Back if you want to change anything.
  2. Tap Report incident to send it.
If you enter the postcode, make sure it is complete (5 digits): a partial one won’t let you send.

And after sending, what next?

The window confirms what happened with one of these messages:

“Incident reported.”Management has it now.
“… Your replacement car request is pending with administration.”Reporting it doesn’t bring the car: administration decides and will let you know.
“Offline: the incident was saved on this device…”It will be sent automatically when you get signal back. Don’t sign out in the meantime.
“Incident reported; an attachment could not be uploaded.”The incident is there, but a file is missing: upload it from Document.
  1. The incident appears on your car’s Incidents card, with its count in the title, until it is closed.
  2. Fleet management handles it: they find a workshop, change the car’s state if needed and, once it is resolved, close it.
  3. If you asked for a replacement car, the request also appears in My profile → Requests, where you can see whether it has been granted or rejected.

Accident FAQs

Should I report it as an accident or as an incident?
If there was a collision (with another car, an object, an animal…), it's an accident. If the car simply fails, with no collision, it's a breakdown incident.
The other driver won't sign the joint accident report form
Don't argue. Note their registration, take photos and, if necessary, call the police. In the app, fill in what you have: only their name and registration are required.
I got something wrong after sending
You can't edit a sent report from the app. Tell your supervisor or fleet management the correct details.
It happened a few days ago and I didn't report it
Report it anyway, with the date and time it happened. The sooner, the better for the insurance.

Upload, request and view documents

The car's paperwork (registration certificate, insurance…) and yours (driving licence…).

What do you need to do?

Nothing is changed or deleted directly from the app: you can upload and view, but fixing, deleting and viewing restricted documents are requested, and fleet management decides. That way no fleet document disappears by mistake.

The “Documentation” card

On the My vehicle screen, scroll down to Documentation and tap it to expand it. The number next to it is how many documents there are in total.

  1. Inside there are two tabs, each with its own count:
    • Vehicle documents: registration certificate, technical datasheet, insurance, contract… They stay with the car when you return it.
    • Driver documents: your personal documents, such as your driving licence. They go with you, and your supervisor and fleet management can see them too. At the bottom you'll find Upload personal document.
  2. Each row shows the type, the day it was uploaded and, if it expires, when it expires.
  3. The coloured badge shows its status:
    ValidIt's all in order.
    ExpiredIts date has passed: you need to upload the new one (or ask for it).
    Pending archivingIt's already in the app and is saved to the company archive later, automatically.
    Deletion pendingSomeone has asked to delete it and fleet management is reviewing it.
  4. Some documents have a line underneath showing your access, for example “View: on request · Download: on request · Deletion: not allowed”. See Request access.
You don't see every document in the fleet: only the ones for your car that fleet management lets drivers see, the ones you uploaded and your personal ones. Missing one? Ask for it.

Ask for a document to be fixed

Did you upload it with the wrong type, the wrong expiry or a note that doesn't fit? No need to delete it and upload it again: ask for it to be fixed.

  1. Tap the row's pencil . “Fix document” opens.
  2. Change what's wrong: the document type, the expiry date (only for documents that expire) or the document note.
  3. In Why it needs fixing, explain it in one sentence.
  4. Press Send request. Management applies or rejects it.
This doesn't change the document right away or touch the file: it only sends the request. In the meantime, the document stays as it was.
A document can only have one request at a time: if you've already asked for a fix, you can't also request deletion until management answers.

Request a document's deletion

  1. Tap the row's bin . “Request deletion · document” opens.
  2. If you like, write the reason (optional): “It's a duplicate”, “It's for another car”…
  3. Press Request deletion.
  4. The document stays in the list with the Deletion pending badge and the bin greyed out, until management decides.

What management can decide

Delete itIt disappears from the list (management keeps it aside in case it needs to be recovered).
Hide itIt stays in the fleet, but is no longer visible in the field app.
RejectNothing changes: it shows as normal again, without the badge.

Request access to a document

Management decides, per document type, what the driver can do with it. That's why some documents are “on request” and others “not allowed”. The line under the name tells you:

The line says…What it means
on requestYou have to ask for it. Instead of the eye or the arrow, a key appears.
requested, awaiting managementYou've already asked: an hourglass appears.
grantedIt's been granted: the normal button is back.
not allowedNot possible, even on request. The button is greyed out.
  1. Tap the key . “Request to view” (or to download) the document opens.
  2. Write what you need it for (optional) and press Send request.
  3. Once management grants it, you'll see the eye or the arrow again.
A greyed-out button isn't broken: tap it and it explains why you can't, as in the “Not allowed” screenshot. If you really need it, talk to whoever runs the fleet.

Document FAQs

I can't find a document I know exists
Fleet management may not have made it visible to drivers. Ask for it from Document → Ask for a document: it also works for ones you can't see in the list.
I chose the wrong type when uploading
Tap the pencil and ask for a fix. Don't delete it and upload it again: you'd lose the original file and its history.
It's said “Pending archiving” for days
The document is already saved and you can view it from the app. Archiving it to the company Drive is retried automatically; if it takes too long, let fleet management know.
When I open it, it says “The document could not be opened”
Check your signal and try again. If it keeps failing with that document, its file may no longer exist: let fleet management know.
Who can see my personal documents?
You, your supervisor and fleet management. Your personal documents are never hidden from you.

What you have pending

“Due soon”, alerts and the colours.

  1. When you open the app, you’ll see “Due soon” at the top if something is missing: this month’s km reading, fuel consumption, an MOT or upcoming maintenance. Tap the notice and its form opens right there.
  2. Expand the Alerts card to see everything open on the car, with its level (Critical Warning) and its date.
  3. Tap Resolve on the alert: usually it means doing what it asks (logging the reading, the MOT, the maintenance…). Once you save it, the alert closes.

What do the colours mean?

Green · all up to date.
Amber · the date is getting close, or you haven’t logged anything for 15-30 days.
Red · overdue, less than 7 days away, or more than 30 days without logging.
If you don’t see the “Due soon” block, you have nothing pending.

My profile: your details and your requests

You don’t edit your record directly: you request the change.

  1. Go to My profile or tap your initials in the header. You’ll see your email, phone, licence type and whether you have a fuel card.
  2. At the bottom, My details opens a window with 3 steps:
    • Your details: correct only what’s wrong.
    • Documents: review your personal documents.
    • Send: check what changes, add a note and send.
  3. Fleet management reviews the request and either applies or rejects it.
  4. Under Requests you see everything you’ve asked for, pending and decided (rejected ones too). Under My documents, your personal papers.
From your profile, the Document button lets you upload, for example, your driving licence.

Running your fleet

If you supervise cars, the “Fleet” view is your dashboard: what you have, what needs attention and how to resolve it without leaving the app.

Supervisor

What do you need to do?

Open “Fleet” and read “In your charge”

  1. Tap Fleet at the top. The bottom bar changes to Home Alerts Km projection.
  2. At the top, Fleet in my care tells you how many vehicles you can see and how many km readings are missing this month.
  3. The In your charge card has two blocks:
    • What you have: Vehicles you supervise and the Drivers who drive them.
    • Alerts and incidents: how many Alerts, Incidents and Accidents are open.
  4. Tap any figure and its list opens. Alerts and incidents are resolved right there, with the same form as everywhere else in the app.
“Vehicles” at the top and “Vehicles” in In your charge may not match: the top one also counts your own car; In your charge counts only the ones you supervise.
You can collapse In your charge by tapping its title, to go straight to the car list.

The car list

  1. Below In your charge you’ll find all your cars, one per card: plate, status, who drives it and its kilometres.
  2. Use the search box (plate, model, driver…) and the status filter to narrow it down.
  3. If you supervise multi-user cars, the list splits into two collapsible groups: General fleet and Multi-user cars. Multi-user cars have no driver: you log their km and fuel.
  4. Each card has its own shortcuts with icons:
    IncidentReport it on the driver’s behalf.
    AccidentThe accident report.
    MaintenanceOpens Update on its maintenance tab.
    ReminderSend reminder to the driver.
    Schedule MOTBook the MOT.
    Schedule serviceChoose its maintenance programme.
  5. Tap the card to open the car record, with all its buttons. See the record.

The car record

Tap a car in the list. At the top you see its km, its upcoming appointments and its projection; if this month’s reading is missing, a notice with Log it now. Below, its buttons:

The “Update” window

Update maintenance opens “Update · plate” on the maintenance tab, and from there you can move to the others: Km, Fuel, MOT (if it has an appointment), Maintenance (if it has a plan) and Incidents (the open ones, with their count, sorted by priority). It’s handy when you have several things for the same car: it doesn’t close when you save, it tells you what it saved and you carry on with another tab.

Update maintenance only opens the window when the maintenance is 30 days away or less. Before that it appears greyed out: tap it and it tells you when it’s due and from what day you can log it.
Responsibility notice. These windows start with an amber box: logging is the driver’s job, and whatever you do is recorded under your name. Close it with the X; it leaves an icon in the window header so you can read it again, and it comes back in your next session.

Schedule the MOT and maintenance

You can book the appointments for your cars yourself. They are two separate buttons on the car card (and on its record):

Schedule MOT

MOT date RequiredThe one the test centre gave you. You can correct it later.
Preferred postcodeOptional: where to look for a test centre from (the area, not the centre). 5 digits.

If it already had an appointment, saving corrects it: there’s only one appointment.

Schedule service

  1. Choose the programme from the shared catalogue (for example General service): it only says every how many km and months it’s due.
  2. Enter from when it counts: the date, the km or both, depending on the programme. You can load the km from the car with one tap.
  3. Save.
A car can only have one scheduled maintenance at a time. If it already has one, the window tells you which instead of showing the form. Removing an appointment is done by administration.
You can also schedule on a car that is covered by a replacement: the MOT and maintenance are still its own.

  2. The window tells you what happened: “Email sent.”, “In-app alert created.” or why the email didn’t go out (the driver has no email, email is disabled…).
If the car has no driver, the window warns you: the email can’t be delivered.

The alerts inbox for your whole fleet

  1. Tap Alerts in the bottom bar.
  2. There are three collapsed cards: Alerts, Incidents and Accidents, each showing how many are open.
  3. Expand one: each row says which car it belongs to. At the top you can search (by plate too, accents optional), filter by type and sort by priority or by date.
  4. Tap Resolve on an alert: whatever it asks for opens (log km, the MOT, the maintenance…). Once you save it, the alert closes.
  5. Show closed shows what’s already resolved.
Sorting by priority is deciding where to start. For an alert, the priority is its level (the app works it out from how close the date is); for an incident, it’s set by whoever opened it.

Km projection: who is going to exceed the contract

  1. Tap Km projection. At the top: how many vehicles, how many to watch and how many at risk.
  2. Filter with the chips: All, Overage risk, On track…
  3. Each card shows the % of km used, the bar (the line marks where it should be given the contract time elapsed), the monthly average against the contracted one, the projection to end of contract and the remaining km. If it’s going to exceed, the estimated overage appears in km and euros.
  4. The chart button shows how the readings have evolved.
  5. If the car has the overage alert, tap Resolve.

Resolving an overage: propose another driver

The window asks “Should someone else drive it?”: propose someone from your fleet who drives less, or choose “Nobody on the list — I explain below” and write the note. Tap Send proposal.

Nothing changes at that moment: administration decides on the proposal in its requests, and the driver change is made by fleet management. The driver doesn’t see this alert.

No signal

Whatever you log without a connection isn’t lost.

  1. If you save something while offline, the app queues it and tells you with an amber notice under the header.
  2. The button for that action shows an amber dot while it waits.
  3. When the connection comes back, it’s sent automatically (or tap the notice to send it now) and the app tells you what was sent.
Don’t log out or clear the browser data while there are items in the queue, or they’ll be lost.

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions.

I keep getting the km email even though I already logged them
Check that the reading was saved and isn’t queued because of no signal. The reminder stops as soon as this month’s reading is logged.
I can’t tap the MOT or maintenance button
They only become active 30 days before the appointment. Tap it anyway: it will tell you the appointment date and from what day you can log it.
I can’t see the MOT button
Your car has no MOT scheduled. Appointments are set by administration or your supervisor.
I entered something wrong on a document
Tap the document’s pencil and request the correction. There’s no need to delete it and upload it again.
Can I change my car’s status to “broken down”?
Not directly. Report an incident and say how the car is left under Availability; fleet management changes the status.
My session suddenly ended
It happens after 2 hours, after a while without using it, or if you signed in with your user on another device. Just sign in again.
Is the app available in English?
Yes: switch the language with ES / EN in the header (also on the sign-in screen).
